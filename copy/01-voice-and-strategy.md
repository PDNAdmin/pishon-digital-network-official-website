# 01: Voice & Strategy
*Decide this first. Every headline on the site traces back to something on this page.*

---

## 1. Who we're talking to

We don't talk to "SMEs" or "businesses looking for digital transformation". We talk to a person running a business who is busy, a bit tired, and has been burned by a web person before.

### Portrait A: Amara, salon owner
- Runs a salon with 4 chairs. Bookings come in through Instagram DMs, WhatsApp and the odd phone call.
- She's doing a client's hair when the messages come in. By the time she replies, the person has booked somewhere else.
- Her "website" is a link-in-bio page with an old price list.
- **What she'd say:** "I lose bookings just because I can't reply fast enough."
- **What she wants:** a full diary without living on her phone.

### Portrait B: Daniel, restaurant owner
- Family restaurant, good reviews, steady regulars.
- His website was built by a cousin 6 years ago. The menu is a PDF that doesn't open on phones.
- People call to ask the same things: opening hours, parking, "do you do gluten-free?", "can I book for 12?"
- **What he'd say:** "People find us on Google, then they leave because the site looks dead."
- **What he wants:** new faces through the door, and the phone to stop ringing with the same five questions.

### Portrait C: Grace, clinic / service business manager
- Runs a small clinic, dental practice, law office, cleaning company or anything else that runs on appointments.
- Calls after 5pm go to voicemail. Most people never leave one.
- She's tried "a chatbot" before. It was useless and customers hated it.
- **What she'd say:** "I don't want a robot annoying my patients. I want someone to actually help them."
- **What she wants:** to stop missing enquiries after hours without hiring another person.

**The thread running through all three:** they're good at what they do, but they're losing customers in the gaps: the slow reply, the website that looks untrustworthy, the call nobody picked up.

---

## 2. The pain bank (in their words)

Use these as raw material for headlines, cards, ads and FAQ. These are lines a real owner would say out loud.

**Missed customers**
- "I miss messages while I'm working."
- "By the time I reply, they've gone somewhere else."
- "My phone rings when I'm with a customer and I can't pick up."
- "Nobody answers after we close, and that's when people message."

**The website**
- "My website is embarrassing. I don't even send people to it."
- "People find us on Google and then leave."
- "It doesn't work properly on phones."
- "I can't update anything without calling someone."
- "I paid for a website and it never brought me a single customer."

**Time & repetition**
- "I'm answering the same ten questions all day."
- "I spend my evenings replying to DMs."
- "I'm the receptionist, the marketer and the boss."

**Trust & past bad experiences**
- "The last web guy disappeared halfway through."
- "I don't understand any of the tech stuff."
- "Chatbots are annoying. My customers will hate it."
- "I don't want to pay for something and not see results."

**What they want (the desire side)**
- "I just want my diary full."
- "I want to look as good online as we actually are."
- "I want to switch my phone off in the evening."
- "I want something that just works, so I don't have to think about it."

## 3. The one-liner

**Recommended:**
> **We make sure you stop losing customers: a website they trust, and an assistant that answers them while you're busy.**

Alternatives for the boss to choose from:
1. "Your customers are trying to reach you. We make sure someone always answers."
2. "Websites that win people's trust, and assistants that reply, book and follow up day and night."
3. "We help busy business owners get found, get trusted and never miss a customer again."

> Everything else on the site is this sentence said differently for each section.

## 4. Why should they believe us? (proof)

A claim with no proof is noise. Every major claim on the site needs one of these behind it. **Do not invent any of it.** Fill in the slots with real material.

| Claim | Proof we'll show | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Our websites bring in customers | Before/after of a real client site + a number (enquiries, bookings, calls) | `[PROOF NEEDED: client name, before/after screenshots, result]` |
| Our assistants answer customers properly | A real (anonymised) conversation transcript from a live agent | `[PROOF NEEDED: 1–2 real chat transcripts]` |
| Clients are happy | Video testimonials, 30–60s, owner on camera in their own business | `[PROOF NEEDED: video links / files]` |
| We're easy to work with | Written testimonial mentioning communication / timelines | `[PROOF NEEDED: quotes + name + business]` |
| We've done this many times | Count of sites / agents launched, industries served | `[PROOF NEEDED: real numbers]` |

**Rule:** a specific number ("31 bookings in the first month") beats a big adjective ("amazing results") every time. If we don't have the number, we show the work and let the client speak.

## 5. Tone of voice

Four words. Each one comes with a description, because the word alone isn't enough.

### Plain-spoken
We say it the way you'd say it across a table. If an owner wouldn't use the word, we don't either.
- **Line:** no jargon, ever, even when explaining how things work. "It replies on WhatsApp," not "omnichannel messaging integration."
- **Sounds like:** "Your website should get you customers. If it doesn't, it's just a nice picture."

### Warm, not cute
We're on their side. We get that running a business is hard. But we don't do jokes at the customer's expense, and we don't use exclamation marks to fake enthusiasm.
- **Line:** friendly in the margins (form success, 404, footer). Clear in the places that matter (hero, pricing, form).
- **Sounds like:** "Go and look after your customers. We'll get back to you by tomorrow."

### Straight
We tell people what it costs, how long it takes and what happens next. No "solutions tailored to your unique needs."
- **Line:** if we don't know, we say so. We never promise results we can't back up.
- **Sounds like:** "Most websites take 2–4 weeks. We'll tell you the exact date before we start."

### Sure
Calm confidence. We've done this before. We don't shout, and we don't beg.
- **Line:** no "best-in-class", no "world-leading." We let the work and the clients make the case.
- **Sounds like:** "Here's what we built for Daniel's restaurant. Here's what happened next."

## 6. Words we don't use

These are words that only exist in marketing. Visitors skim past them, and they make a site sound like every other agency (or like it was written by AI).

| Don't write | Write instead |
|---|---|
| solutions | (say the actual thing) a website, an assistant |
| elevate / take to the next level | get more customers, look better, save time |
| leverage | use |
| seamless | it just works / no fuss |
| cutting-edge / innovative / state-of-the-art | (cut it, or show it) |
| empower | help / let you |
| curated / bespoke | made for you / built around how you work |
| digital presence | your website / how people find you online |
| robust / scalable | it won't break / it grows with you |
| unlock | get |
| harness the power of AI | (cut it) "it replies to your customers for you" |
| in today's fast-paced world | (cut it) |
| we're passionate about | (show it, don't say it) |
| conversational AI / LLM / automation workflows | assistant / it answers / it handles it for you |

**Formatting rules (anti-AI-sounding):**
- No em dashes in site copy. Use a full stop or a comma.
- No "It's not just X, it's Y" constructions.
- No lists of three adjectives ("fast, reliable and secure").
- No exclamation marks except in the form success message (one, maximum).
- Read every line out loud. If it sounds like an ad voiceover, rewrite it.

## 7. Different parts of the site, different jobs

| Where | Job | Clarity or charm? |
|---|---|---|
| Hero, section headlines | Understood in 3 seconds | **Clarity** |
| Buttons / CTAs | Say exactly what happens on click | **Clarity** ("Book a free call", not "Let's go") |
| Sales page form | Feels quick and safe | **Clarity** |
| Prices / timelines | Straight answer | **Clarity** |
| Form success, 404, footer, loading states, hover text | Make people like us | **Charm** |
| FAQ | Answer the worry behind the question | Clarity, warm tone |

Example of charm in the margins:
- 404: "This page took the day off. Our assistant didn't. [Go home]"
- Form success: "Got it. We'll reply within one working day. Probably sooner."
- Footer: "Made by people who answer their messages."
