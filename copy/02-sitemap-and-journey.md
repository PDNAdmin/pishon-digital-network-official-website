# 02: Sitemap & Journey

## Sitemap

```
/                 Home: pain, what we do, show it working, proof, CTA
/websites         Websites: "A website people trust, that brings in customers"
/ai-assistants    AI Assistants: "Someone who answers your customers, day and night"
/work             Our work: case studies (before/after + results + client video)
/work/[client]    Single case study
/about            Who we are: real faces, how we work, what we promise
/contact          SALES PAGE (ad landing page + contact page in one)
```

Nav (5 items max): **Websites · AI Assistants · Our work · About** + button **[Book a free call]** → `/contact`

> We call the service "AI Assistants" in the nav, not "AI Agents". "Agent" is an industry word. "Assistant" is a word owners already use.

---

## The homepage journey (modelled on SpinX, told in the customer's words)

Each step answers the question the visitor has at that moment.

| # | Visitor is thinking… | Section answers with… |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Is this for me?" | **Hero**: names their pain in one line |
| 2 | "Do they get my problem?" | **Sound familiar?**: pain cards in their words |
| 3 | "What do they actually do?" | **Two doors**: Website / Assistant, framed as outcomes |
| 4 | "Does it really work?" | **See it working**: interactive chat demo + before/after slider |
| 5 | "Is this complicated?" | **How it works**: 3 plain steps |
| 6 | "Has it worked for anyone like me?" | **Proof**: results + video testimonials |
| 7 | "But what about…?" | **FAQ / worries** |
| 8 | "OK, what now?" | **Final CTA** → `/contact` |

---

## How every page leads to the sales page

- **Every page** ends with the same closing CTA block → `/contact`.
- **Nav button** "Book a free call" is always visible (sticky on scroll).
- **Service pages** link to `/contact?for=website` or `/contact?for=assistant` so the sales page opens with the matching headline.
- **Case studies** end with "Want this for your business?" → `/contact?for=<service used>`.
- **Ads** link straight to `/contact?for=...&utm_...`. The sales page works on its own; ad visitors never need to see the homepage.

## Paid ad traffic flow

```
Ad (Instagram / Facebook / Google / TikTok)
   │  promise in the ad = headline on the page
   ▼
/contact?for=assistant | website | both
   │  hero + short form above the fold
   │  proof → offer → worries → form again
   ▼
Form submitted → success state → (optional) book a time on calendar
   ▼
We reply within 1 working day (WhatsApp / email / call: their choice)
```
