# 04: Contact = Sales Page (`/contact`)

**This page does two jobs:**
1. It's where every paid ad lands, so it has to convert a cold stranger on its own.
2. It's the normal "Contact" page for people already on the site.

**The golden rule: message match.** The headline must repeat the promise the ad made. If the ad says "Never miss a booking again", the page can't open with "Websites that convert."

---

## Variant switching (build note)

`/contact?for=assistant` · `/contact?for=website` · `/contact?for=both` (default, used when no parameter is given)

Only the **hero headline, subhead, the "what you get" list and the form's pre-selected option** change. Everything else stays the same. Keep UTM parameters and pass them into the form submission as hidden fields.

## 2. The cost of doing nothing

### Headline
## Every slow reply is a customer you paid to get, then lost.

### Body
You already spend money and time getting people to notice you: ads, posts, word of mouth. Then they message, and wait. Or they visit your website, and it doesn't load properly on their phone. So they go somewhere else.

### Three quick facts (pain, not stats)
- **They message when you're busy.** Mid-appointment, mid-service, mid-dinner.
- **They don't wait.** Most people contact two or three businesses and go with whoever replies first.
- **They judge in seconds.** An old, slow or broken website makes a great business look closed.

## 8. Worries (FAQ, sales-focused)

**"Is the call actually free?"**
Yes. 20 minutes, no card, no commitment. If we can't help, we'll tell you.

**"Will you try to hard-sell me?"**
No. You'll get a plan and a price. What you do with it is up to you.

**"I'm not good with technology."**
That's fine. We do the setup. You'll only need to know a couple of things, and we'll show you.

**"Will the assistant say something wrong to my customers?"**
It only answers using the information you approve. When it's not sure, it passes the conversation to you instead of guessing.

**"Do I need a new website to get an assistant?"**
No. It can work on your existing site, WhatsApp and Instagram.

**"How much does it cost?"**
It depends on what you need, which is why we give you a fixed price after the call. `[OPTIONAL: price ranges]`

**"How quickly can it be ready?"**
Assistants: usually 1–2 weeks. Websites: usually 2–4 weeks.

## Build & tracking notes (for the Next.js build later)
- Fire analytics events: `form_start`, `form_submit`, `form_success`, `whatsapp_click`, `call_click`, `video_play`, plus a scroll-depth event at 50% and 90%.
- Meta Pixel `Lead` and Google Ads conversion on `form_success`.
- Store UTM parameters + `for` variant with each lead.
- The page must load fast on mobile data (target LCP < 2.5s). Lazy-load the video, keep the hero static.
- Accessible form: real `<label>`s, visible focus states, errors announced to screen readers.
